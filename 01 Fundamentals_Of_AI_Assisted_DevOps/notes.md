## Generative AI

Primary use case is to generate content. <br>
generate text, images, videos etc. <br> <br>

Ex: Ask Generative AI Model to generate image of women sitting on cliff and eating ice cream. <br> <br>

Text generating AI Models are called LLMs. <br>
So, Large Language Models are part of Gen AI which is focused on text generation. <br> <br>

In DevOps, <br>
to generate a k8s manifest, it is something that is not available on internet. <br>
Ask LLMs to generatr k8s manifest, it starts writing, it doesnot copy from stack overflow or from github repo etc, it generates a complete new manifest. <br>
